## Who knocks (canon fit)

Woken Remnants freed elsewhere — the wake sticks, the market moves contracts, and word of the
Kettleworks travels. A doorstep fighter arrives *already awake*, carrying their old job and a
long road. (Rarely, one who was never taken at all.) They are nobody's rescue: they chose you.

## The beat, in order

**1. The knock** (parchment toast at the hub — understatement is the thrill):

> *Someone is at the door of {gym_name}.*

**2. The doorstep card** (storyteller narration → spoken line):

> The knock came {time_flavour}. On the step stood {a_chassis_line} — **{name}**, who was
> built to {job}, and who had walked a long way to stand at this particular door.
>
> **"{spoken_line}"**

**3. The scout's read** (parchment block — potential-as-rarity, bantam #5):

- No Scout staff: `{name} · {stars_wide}` — *"Hard to say, by lamplight."*
- Waymark woken: `{name} · {stars_tight}` — *"Waymark says: {scout_line}"*

**4. The choice** (two buttons, both kind):

- **Open the door** → *The kettle was on before the latch was. {name} hangs their road-dust by
  the fire. The board has a new name on it.*
- **Send them on, kindly** → *You wrap a coal in cloth and point the warm way through the dark.
  Some doors open later.* (May return in a weekly pool — tuning knob.)
- Roster full (replaces both): **The board is full** → *{name} reads the five names slowly, nods
  like a door closing softly, and leaves a chalk mark on the post — the walkers' sign for
  "good hearth."* (Return rules — tuning knob.)

## Conditioning slots (text only — art never varies)

`{gym_name}` · `{time_flavour}` (after closing / in the rain / the night after a loss — the last
one only when lamps were recently lost, for maximum tenderness) · `{a_chassis_line}` (one-line

## Open question for the owner (not a tuning knob — a canon call)

Do doorstep signees add a **fireside cup**? Canon counts cups for friends *you* freed or woke;
a doorstep arrival woke elsewhere. Options: (a) no cup — cups stay strictly yours-to-earn;
(b) they bring **their own cup** with them, chipped, from wherever they were freed — the wall
grows either way and the object tells their story. (b) is recommended: it keeps the wall as
"everyone home", and a carried, chipped cup is a free story beat.
